# TubePulse terms of use

TubePulse is free, open-source software distributed under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). The license explains the permissions for using, modifying and distributing TubePulse and the terms under which it is provided.

TubePulse lets you choose which current YouTube playback details to share through Discord. You control when sharing is enabled and can turn it off from the extension. Discord controls who can see the activity according to your Discord settings. See the [TubePulse privacy policy](privacy.md) for details.

Your use of [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Discord](https://discord.com/terms) is subject to their respective terms. TubePulse is an independent project.

For questions about TubePulse, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/TheLegendaryLord/tubepulse/issues).
